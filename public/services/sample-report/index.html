In plain termsWe put a quantum algorithm and a classical one on the same investment problem, same time, same machine. The classical one found the perfect answer in all twenty tests; the quantum one always fell short — between 10% and 87% depending on the seed — and the gap does not shrink consistently as the problem grows. The question stays open for one measured reason: the classical solver's time to prove it found the best answer grew about 25× for every four assets added.

The five layers

0 · INTEGRITY

Harness harness_v0.py published with the record; instances regenerated from declared seeds; both sides run on the same machine under a 120 s budget.

1 · VERDICT

Criterion sealed before any run: advantage = QAOA reaching the exact optimum at least as often as CP-SAT within budget. Result: never.

2 · MAGNITUDE

Mean gaps 48.2 / 25.1 / 41.1 % with seed dispersion 10–23 points; every one of the 20 individual gaps is drawn below and recomputed from the sealed runs.

3 · ROBUSTNESS

Seed ranges overlap across n=12/16/20; the verdict does not depend on any single seed or size. QAOA never wins against a strong baseline; whether it wins against weak ones was not the question.

4 · TRIGGERS

Measured side note: CP-SAT's optimality-proof time grew ~25× per +4 assets (0.05 s → 1.3 s → 29 s). If that curve continues, the budget-parity fight at n ≥ 28 is a different fight.

5 · YOUR UNITS

Set with the client: the gap translated into the rebalancing book, frequency and cost of suboptimality — supplied by the client or marked as assumption.
