## What it means

An **advantaged solve** is the honest unit of value in quantum computing: one problem, solved, where the quantum result *measurably beat the strongest classical solver the user already runs* — on the same instance, with the same budget, verified with raw evidence.

## Why the definition is strict on purpose

Most quantum claims are measured in the wrong unit: qubits, gate counts, or a paper's asymptotic speedup. None of those tell a buyer whether *their* problem got solved better. An advantaged solve fixes the unit to the only thing that matters commercially — beating the baseline you run today.

## What it is not

- Not compute time billed by the second.
- Not a theoretical speedup that appears only at sizes no hardware can reach.
- Not a win against a deliberately weak classical baseline. A weak baseline invalidates the claim.

## What we know / what we don't

We know the definition is the right unit to bill and to trust. We do **not** yet claim any advantaged solves exist at useful scale for optimization — as of July 2026 the honest state is that strong classical solvers still win most such problems. That is a measurement to publish, not a fact to hide.
